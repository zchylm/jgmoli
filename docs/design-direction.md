# Design Direction

JG MOLI is a gaming-first platform that helps players build a better setup around what they already own, how they play, their room and their budget. A cockpit is one premium category, not the brand's centre.

## Editorial gaming experience

The working direction combines:

- a warm, light interface with generous editorial space;
- large gaming-space photography as the first visual priority;
- concise brand language rather than hardware-style readouts;
- controlled dark imagery for atmosphere instead of an all-dark website;
- complete gaming spaces as the visual centre, with specialist systems shown in context;
- lighter typography, quiet navigation and slow transitions;
- light and dark sections chosen to support the content they contain.

JG MOLI should feel like a modern gaming-lifestyle brand. Its identity comes from spaces, relationships between equipment and visual storytelling—not JON.PC-style hardware density or generic gaming decoration.

## Home-page priorities

The first design pass deliberately concentrates on three sections:

1. Hero — establish the brand promise and let visitors switch between competitive, immersive and racing scenes.
2. Find Your Starting Point — separate the user's current platform from the experience they want.
3. Explore How You Play — introduce complete gaming experiences before individual equipment.

The Setup Builder will begin as a two-dimensional visual composition backed by explicit compatibility rules. It will not imply spatial accuracy or product compatibility from an image alone.

## Product source of truth

Explore Gaming Gear, Shop by Experience and Build My Setup must read from one product model. Product basics, price and availability, specifications, compatibility, experience tags and recommendation tags must not be maintained separately for each shopping path.

The current scene imagery is selected reference material from the supplied library. It is not approved product photography or a statement of final product capability.
